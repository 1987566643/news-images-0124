One image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 — a broadsheet front page with 40 dated stories across 7 regions: NATO's vow to defend space against a reported Russian nuclear-armed satellite, Ukraine's sea-drone strike sinking the Russian corvette Merkuriy off Libya, three tankers hit by sea mines in the Strait of Hormuz, the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang as chief executive, and a North Korean jet entering South Korean airspace — plus the day's other stories, from the COP31 fallout and the EU's 22nd sanctions package to the Sanofi–Insmed deal, the Nepal earthquake, and the 2031 Women's World Cup hosts. The full verified story list is in NEWS.md. The image is drawn from scratch by `main.py` (pure Python standard library: a hand-written PNG encoder and an embedded 5x7 bitmap font), so every headline and caption is crisp, legible text and the program runs offline with nothing installed.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
